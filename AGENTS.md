## Hardware design the proposal locks in
If firmware or schematics are added later, these are binding constraints from the proposal:
- ESP32 DevKit: GPIO34 = LDR divider ADC (3.3 V max — never feed 5 V into it), GPIO27 = PIR OUT, GPIO26 = MOSFET gate.
- Lamp turns on only when it is dark AND motion is detected; after the last motion it stays on for a delay, then off.
- Logic-level N-MOSFET as low-side switch (gate 3.3 V, source to GND, drain to LED negative); all grounds common. Check the PIR's OUT voltage before wiring it to GPIO27.
- During measurement use one regulated 5 V source and disconnect the laptop programming USB — never two sources on the board, and never >12 V or mains.

## SMARTLUX v2 features (2026-11-21)
The proposal now documents four additional features; the firmware and enclosure must match it.
- **Pin map additions**: I²C SDA GPIO21, SCL GPIO22 (OLED SSD1306 0x3C and INA219 0x40 share the bus); override button GPIO32 with `INPUT_PULLUP`, other leg to GND, active LOW, 50 ms debounce. Existing GPIO34/27/26 are unchanged.
- **Override state machine**: short press 50–800 ms cycles `AUTO → MANUAL_ON → MANUAL_OFF → AUTO`; long press >1500 ms returns to `AUTO`. AUTO keeps the original dark-AND-motion logic plus delay-off. Manual modes ignore the sensors and have no auto-timeout; the dashboard has an AUTO button.
- **Energy measurement**: INA219 is high-side on the 5 V rail (supply → VIN+ → VIN− → ESP32 5V + PIR + LED rail), 0.1 Ω shunt, ±3.2 A. Firmware integrates `E_Wh += V·I·dt/3600` every second and separately estimates lamp energy and savings against an always-on lamp.
- **Dashboard**: STA credentials live in the git-ignored `firmware/SMARTLUX/secrets.h` (commit only `secrets.example.h`); AP fallback SSID `SMARTLUX-XXXX` after 20 s STA failure; mDNS `smartlux.local`; endpoints `GET /`, `GET /api/status`, `GET /api/override?mode=auto|on|off`, `GET /api/reset`. No CDN, no `delay()` in `loop()`.
- **Enclosure**: parametric `hardware/enclosure/smartlux_case.scad` (case + lid, cutouts for OLED/button/PIR dome/LDR window/USB/DC jack, board standoffs, vents). Do not commit binary STL files; `*.stl` is git-ignored.
